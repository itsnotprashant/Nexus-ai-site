Design study — real-time WebGL

The high-top,
built in the browser

A deconstructed high-top sneaker modelled entirely in code. No mesh files, no image assets — the silhouette comes from a handful of profile curves swept into lofted surfaces, and every panel, seam and stitch is painted into a canvas texture at load time. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Silhouette

Three monotone cubic curves — width, height and collar opening — are sampled 220 times from heel to toe. Each sample becomes a closed cross-section, and the stack of sections is triangulated into one surface.

Collar & throat

The opening is cut by letting each section fold back down an inner wall to the insole, so the shoe is a genuine hollow shell — look straight down into it and you see the lining.

Panels & type

The UV layout falls out of the sweep, so panels, exposed double stitching, eyelets, perforations and the midsole wordmark are all drawn with the 2D canvas API and swapped live per colourway.

Parts

Midsole, upper, padded collar, tongue and laces are separate groups — which is what makes the exploded view possible. Laces and the zip tie are swept tubes along spline paths.
